Privacy notice

Your information

Last updated: 25 September 2026

What we collect

From productions: contact details and the casting brief you submit (project type, dates, headcount, look, and job notes). From talent: the profile details you choose to provide so we can match you to briefs. We also store the page URL and campaign tags if present. We do not use detailed browser fingerprinting.

Why we use it

Briefs are used to confirm requirements and prepare a shortlist. Talent cards are used to assess fit for specific jobs. An inbound request is not marketing consent. Marketing permission must be a separate, optional choice.

Who sees it

Authorised Runway personnel see briefs and talent cards. A production receives only the shortlist Runway sends for that job — not the full book. Hosting providers may process information only to keep the service running.

What we do not collect here

The public forms do not collect ID numbers, bank details, or copies of identity documents. Talent must be 18 or older to use this form. Applications for minors are not accepted here.

Photos and usage

Portfolio links and photos you send are used for casting match and for sharing a card with a production on a specific brief. They are not a general advertising licence.

Retention and choices

Records are kept only as long as needed for matching, booking, legal and operational purposes. You may ask for access, correction or deletion through the same email or WhatsApp channel used for your submission.

Contact

Use the email or WhatsApp conversation associated with your submission. No telephone number is published on this website.
