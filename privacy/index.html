Privacy notice

Your information

Last updated: 25 September 2026

What we collect

From productions: contact details and the casting brief you submit. From talent: the card details you choose to provide so we can match you to briefs.

Why we use it

Briefs confirm requirements and prepare a shortlist. Talent cards assess fit for a specific job. An inbound request is not marketing consent.

Who sees it

Authorised Runway personnel see briefs and talent cards. A production receives only the shortlist for that job — not the full book.

What we do not collect here

The public forms do not collect ID numbers, bank details, or copies of identity documents. Talent must be 18 or older.

Photos and usage

Portfolio links and photos you send are used for casting match and for sharing a card with a production on a specific brief.

Contact

Use the email or WhatsApp conversation associated with your submission. No telephone number is published on this website.
